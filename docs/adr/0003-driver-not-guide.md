# The Station Agent only Shows or Clarifies

Open-ended Commands are still requests to be taken somewhere. The agent does not answer from page copy, and it is not a general assistant. “What is Arkiv?” Shows Arkiv; the lede is already on the page. “How much?” Clarifies toward contact. A talking brochure is a later product.

The AI Voice Nav showcase requirements extend page actions to back through action history, next section in full page order, contact CTA focus, and explicitly opening the existing WhatsApp link. Next uses the currently visible section and records a new Show, including Hero → System → Products and the final Clients → Contact transition; it does not replay the next history entry or stop at the product list. Video demos are excluded. The agent still does not answer product questions or accept arbitrary URLs/selectors. See the current Page Action definition in `CONTEXT.md`.

Business needs and recommendation questions now select the best matching Section even without naming a product or requesting navigation. The catalog includes product capabilities, differences, and System services grounded in the page. Multiple relevant products do not force clarification: specific needs take priority, with BikinKonten as the starting recommendation for general promotion or sales growth. This changes selection, not presentation: one Show, no explanatory text or speech. The validator still rejects unsupported actions and never turns a clarification's first hypothesis into a Show. Contact and opening WhatsApp still require explicit requests.
